Agentic AI development

Agents that plan, act and check their work

An agent is more than a prompt. It plans, calls tools, remembers context, and knows when to ask a person. We engineer these systems for production: observable, testable, and safe to let near real data.

The challenge

Most agent demos break the first week in production

A single model call can look brilliant in a demo. In production the same system has to handle bad inputs, slow tools, partial failures, ambiguous requests and users who change their minds mid-task.

We treat an agent as a distributed system with a language model in it. That means explicit state, idempotent actions, evaluation before release, and a clear boundary between what the agent may do alone and what needs a human decision.

  • Explicit control flow. Plans, handoffs and loops you can read, not hidden in a prompt.
  • Safe actions. Tool calls are validated, idempotent and logged.
  • Grounded answers. Retrieval over your data with sources attached.
  • Measured quality. Evaluation sets and tracing before and after every change.

FAQ

Questions we hear

What is the difference between a chatbot and an agent?

A chatbot answers. An agent decides what to do next: it can plan multi-step work, call tools and APIs, read and write data, and loop until a goal is met or it needs a person.

Which models do you use?

We choose per task from leading providers such as OpenAI, Google and Anthropic, and design the system so models can be swapped as the field changes.

How do you keep agents from doing damage?

Actions go through validated tool interfaces with least-privilege access, idempotency keys and audit logs, and sensitive steps are routed to a human for approval.

Can you work with our existing stack?

Yes. We integrate with your databases, APIs and identity provider, and deploy to your cloud account.

How do we start?

A technical discovery call to define the use case, the data and the risks, followed by a scoped plan.
