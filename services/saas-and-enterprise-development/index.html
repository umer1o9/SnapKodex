The challenge

Early architecture decisions become expensive later

Tenancy, identity and billing touch every part of a product. Getting them wrong early means rewriting data models and permissions once customers are already using the system.

We build the foundations first and in proportion: enough structure to scale and sell to larger customers, without over-engineering a product that is still finding its market.

  • Right-sized architecture. A modular core you can grow into.
  • Enterprise-ready. SSO, audit logging and role-based access from the start.
  • Operable. CI/CD, observability and infrastructure as code.
  • Owned by you. Code, infrastructure and documentation are yours.

FAQ

Questions we hear

Can you take over an existing codebase?

Yes. We start with a review of the code, infrastructure and risks, then agree a plan to stabilise and extend it.

Who owns the code?

You do. Repositories, infrastructure definitions and documentation are handed over as part of the work.

Do you build mobile apps?

We build web applications and APIs, and can support mobile clients through those APIs.

Fixed price or time and materials?

Both are possible. Well-defined scopes can be fixed-price; evolving products usually suit a retained team.

How do you estimate?

A short discovery produces a scope, risks and an estimate range, which we refine as unknowns are resolved.
