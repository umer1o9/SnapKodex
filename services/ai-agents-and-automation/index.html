AI agents and automation

AI that handles the repetitive work and hands off the rest

Customer service, sales, ecommerce and back-office work all contain tasks that follow rules and patterns. We build agents and automations that complete them reliably and escalate cleanly when a person is needed.

The challenge

Automation fails when it ignores the exceptions

Most processes are 80 percent routine and 20 percent exceptions, and the exceptions are where customers get frustrated. An automation that cannot recognise its own limits creates more work than it removes.

We design for the handover: the agent does what it can do well, shows its sources, and passes the conversation to a person with full context when it cannot.

  • Grounded in your content. Answers drawn from your knowledge base, with sources.
  • Connected to your systems. Orders, tickets, CRM and inventory, not just chat.
  • Clean escalation. Context-rich handover to people.
  • Measured. Resolution, handover and satisfaction tracked from day one.

FAQ

Questions we hear

Will the agent replace my support team?

It handles routine requests and drafts replies, so your team spends time on complex and high-value cases. You decide how much to automate.

How do you stop the agent making things up?

Answers are grounded in your content with citations, the agent declines when it does not know, and quality is measured on real conversations.

Which channels do you support?

Web chat, email, messaging apps and internal tools such as Slack, depending on your setup.

Can I see it working before committing?

Yes. We can prepare a live demo on a sample of your content.

How is it priced?

Typically a build phase followed by usage and support. We scope it after a discovery conversation.
