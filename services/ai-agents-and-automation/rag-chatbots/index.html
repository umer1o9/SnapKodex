A chatbot that answers from your documents

Grounded chatbots for customers or staff: they retrieve from your content, cite sources and say when they do not know.

Overview

How we approach it

Public chatbots invent answers. A grounded chatbot retrieves passages from your approved content first and answers only from them, linking to the source.

We build for both customer-facing and internal use, with access control so staff chatbots only surface what each person may see.

Content quality is the ceiling

A chatbot is only as good as the content behind it. We report the gaps so you can close them.

  • Fallback to a human or a form when unsure.
  • Content freshness through scheduled re-indexing.
  • Analytics on top questions.
