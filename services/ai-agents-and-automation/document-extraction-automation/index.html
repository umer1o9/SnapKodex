Document & email extraction

Turn PDFs and emails into structured data

Invoices, contracts, forms and inboxes become clean records. We combine extraction, validation and review so data entry disappears.

Request a live agent demoAI agents & automation
Overview

How we approach it

Large language models can read messy documents, but extraction needs checks: required fields, totals that add up and values that match your records.

We build pipelines that ingest from email or storage, extract fields, validate them, send doubtful items for quick human review and write the results to your systems.

Verify before you write

Extracted values are validated against rules and existing data before they land in your systems.

  • Confidence scores per field.
  • Learning from reviewer corrections.
  • Audit trail from source document to record.

FAQ

Common questions

How accurate is extraction?

It depends on the documents. We measure on your samples and set review thresholds accordingly.

Can it read handwriting or scans?

Often, with OCR and vision models, with lower confidence handled by review.

Where does the data go?

Into the systems you specify, with options for private deployment.

See an agent working on your content

Share a sample of your support or sales material. We will show you a live agent grounded in it.
