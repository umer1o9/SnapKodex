Ontology explorer

A concept graph that people govern

Themes and concepts connect to verses through an ontology. Taaweel treats that graph as something to be curated: machine-proposed links go through automated checks and human adjudication before they are trusted.

Open the app →Taaweel overview
Overview

What it does

The ontology labels verses and relates concepts to each other. Language models can propose new edges and labels, but proposals are not accepted by default. They enter a queue.

Reviewers can approve, reject, remap or merge. Alongside that human step, a policy engine checks structure: no self-loops, cycle checks, subtree constraints, and minimum confidence and provenance criteria.

Governance needs a policy

The tooling supports review, but what to approve is a scholarly decision. Institutions using the ontology should define who may adjudicate and what is uploaded to external AI platforms.

  • Run and worker logs make labelling traceable.
  • Admin tools exist for controlled data reset, to be wrapped in an organisation's own procedures.
  • Role-based access separates ordinary users from administrators.

FAQ

Common questions

Is the ontology complete?

No. It grows through labelling runs and review, and is designed to be extended under governance.

Can I export the graph?

Graph data lives in the application database and is available through its API for authorised administrators.

Who can change the ontology?

Administrators and designated reviewers under role-based access.
