The problem

One-shot AI answers are the wrong tool for Quranic research

A general-purpose chatbot gives a fluent answer and hides how it got there. For research that is a defect: you cannot see what was counted, which sources were consulted, which readings were rejected or why.

Taaweel replaces the single answer with a pipeline. Each stage is separate, saved and resumable, so a researcher can inspect the question as understood, the verses that were found, the commentary that was read, the objections that were raised and the rulings that were made on them.

  • Transparent. Intermediate outputs are kept per session, not thrown away.
  • Evidence first. Every [E-n] citation in a report resolves to a row in the evidence ledger.
  • Self-critical. A red-team stage and a principles audit try to break the conclusion before you see it.
  • Honest about status. Claims carry labels: mainstream, minority view, author position, provisional.

How it works

How a research run works

Refine the question

A short dialogue sharpens the question and fixes its scope before any compute is spent.

Map the corpus

Every occurrence of the key roots and terms is found and clustered into patterns of use.

Collect evidence

Verses, lexicon entries, tafsir positions and hadith are gathered into a tiered ledger.

Test the readings

The inherited reading and its alternatives are tried against linguistic, contextual and cross-verse tests.

Audit and attack

An adversarial stage raises objections; the audit checks the result against 14 principles.

Compose the report

Answer first, then the argument, with every claim cited and every limit stated.

Principles

What Taaweel will and will not claim

  • It assists, it does not rule. Output supports scholarly work; it is not a fatwa and does not replace scholarly authority.
  • It states uncertainty. Language models are probabilistic. Reports label provisional claims and keep the objections visible.
  • Comparison is not identity. When a reading is compared with scientific findings, the report calls it a comparison.
  • No borrowed credentials. Taaweel makes no claim of external certification.
FAQ

Questions people ask

Is Taaweel available now?

Yes. Taaweel is live at taaweel.snapkodex.com. Email hello@snapkodex.com with any questions about what it covers today.

Which sources does it read?

The Quran corpus with root and token data, the Lane lexicon and classical dictionary entries, and tafsir databases including al-Ṭabarī and Ibn Kathīr. Corpora are opened read-only.

How is it different from asking a chatbot?

A chatbot answers once and does not show its work. Taaweel runs staged research, keeps every intermediate result, cites every claim to a ledger of evidence and runs an adversarial audit before writing the report.

Does Taaweel give religious rulings?

No. It is a research tool. It labels the epistemic status of each claim and is designed to support, not replace, qualified scholarship.

Can I export reports?

Yes. Reports can be exported as Markdown, HTML and DOCX.

What is Mizan?

Mizan is a companion application for transparent numerical analysis of the Quran text. It shows what was counted, how, and what the result does not establish.
