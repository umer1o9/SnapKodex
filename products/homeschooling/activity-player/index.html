Activity player

One link, two views: parent and child

When a parent opens an activity, they see how to run it. When the same link is opened signed out, for example on a child's device, it shows only the activity itself.

Open the app →Homeschooling overview
Overview

What it does

The parent view includes instructions in the parent's mother-tongue transliteration, an example of what the activity should look like, a scoring panel and a place to record observations.

The child view is simple and focused. For reading activities it shows words and letters in large type, lets children enlarge or shrink the text, and speaks any word they tap. For Arabic it can use a pre-downloaded library of verses with qirā'āt word by word or verse by verse.

The child view is deliberately limited

A signed-out visitor sees the activity content only. Instructions, scoring and observations stay on the signed-in parent view.

  • No sign-in is needed for the child view; it contains no parent content.
  • Celebration moments mark finished activities.
  • Voice and speech options can be tuned for the child.
How it works

Step by step

Open the link

Parent sees guidance

Instructions, an example, scoring and notes.

Large text, pictures and audio.

Score

The parent records completion when done.

Library audio word by word or verse by verse, with pacing handled for you.

Parent-only guidance

Instructions, examples and scoring hidden from the child's view.

Sibling participation

Activities with two children can carry shared or driving-child scoring.

FAQ

Common questions

Does the child need an account?

No. Opening the activity link while signed out gives the child view.

Where does the Quran audio come from?

A shared platform library of Quran and Noorani Qaida audio that is attached and paced automatically.

Can I change the voice?

Yes. Voice selection is available for the player, including different voices for scenes.
