Fair scoring and observations

Reward finishing, never ranking

The platform's assessment rule is that a child is not compared to a sibling. Scores record whether the assigned task was completed, not how well it ranked. Co-op activities are built so that children win together.

Overview

What it does

Each activity has a scoring configuration. Points or tokens accumulate in a ledger that children can see, using atomic updates so balances stay correct. A reward store lets families decide what points can buy.

Three co-op modes cover sibling work. In a shared result, both children receive one combined score. In an interdependent block, the Shared Success lock means neither child is paid if a breakdown or blame-shifting occurs. In a driving-child activity, child A teaches child B and A is scored on whether B can answer the parent's questions.

Why completion?

Scoring on excellence rewards the child who is already ahead and discourages the one who is not. Scoring on completion rewards effort that anyone can give, and keeps siblings on the same side.

  • Competition, where used, is channelled into personal-best scores only.
  • No curve and no sibling leaderboard.
  • Observations feed analysis of a day and the next plan.

Features

Scoring and noticing

Token ledger

A transparent balance of points across daily subjects and chores, redeemable in a family reward store.

Shared Success

In co-op blocks neither child is rewarded if the partnership breaks down.

Driving-child scoring

The target child's score depends on how well the sibling performs.

Parent observations

Notes about each child during activities, recorded against the observing parent.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I turn off the token store?

Scoring is configured per activity and per family, so you can use the parts that fit your approach.

Who can see observations?

They are recorded against the parent who wrote them within the family's private workspace.

Can the AI use the scores?

Agents can analyse a day's scores and observations to suggest changes, always as a preview.
