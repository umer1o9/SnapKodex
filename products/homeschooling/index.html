The problem

Homeschooling runs on the parent's time and energy

Most families start with a clear vision and then lose weeks to planning: choosing subjects, finding activities, balancing two or three children at different levels, and remembering what was already covered.

The platform treats planning as the work AI should take over, and keeps the parent where they matter: setting direction, teaching, observing and deciding. The family's own philosophy, written in their own words, steers everything the agents produce.

  • Your philosophy leads. The main guiding light, whatever it is, weighs most heavily on the curriculum.
  • Each child is different. Activities are written for the specific child, from basic to deeper over the six months.
  • Scoring is fair. It measures completing the task, not excellence, and never ranks one child against another.
  • Your data stays yours. Families are isolated tenants and AI works only inside the family's own data.

Principles

Design principles you can rely on

  • Tenant isolation. No family can read or write another family's data.
  • Completion over excellence. Scores record whether a task was done, not how it ranks.
  • AI inside the lines. Agents act only within the family and the role of the person using them, and every change is logged.
  • Preview before change. Destructive batch changes are shown for approval first.
FAQ

Questions people ask

Can I start using it now?

Yes. The platform is live and families can sign in and set up their workspace.

Does it replace me as the teacher?

No. It plans, prepares and tracks. Parents teach, score and observe, and the player gives parents their own instructions.

What if my family's approach is not mainstream?

That is the point of the guiding light. Whatever you write there, whether a text, a tradition, a person or an education system, steers the curriculum direction.

How do you handle siblings of different ages?

Activities are assigned per child. You can schedule one-to-one coaching for one child while the others work independently, and add group activities for everyone.

Do children have to be on screens all day?

No. Many activities are hands-on: physical materials, outdoor tasks, teaching a sibling. The parent view carries the instructions.

Is children's data private?

Each family is an isolated tenant. Security rules at the database and checks in the server enforce that access, and AI agents act only inside the family's data.
