Guiding-light curriculum

A curriculum that starts from your philosophy

Most curriculum tools start from a grade level. This one starts from a question: what is your family's main guiding light? The answer shapes the whole plan.

Open the app →Homeschooling overview
Overview

What it does

Set up the family profile with a free-text main guiding light. It might be a scripture, a tradition, a relative's example or an education system. Add guardian profiles (mother tongue, education budget, location, goals) and child profiles (strengths, weaknesses, comments).

Then the curriculum agent starts a conversation. It asks about your vision now and in the long run, suggests directions based on the children and the parents' own skills, and only then produces a six-month plan. Each curriculum states its objectives, content, instruction methods and assessment, and records the guiding light it was built from.

Your values, not ours

The agent does not judge the guiding light you choose. It guides the plan toward it and suggests directions that fit your children and your circumstances.

  • Curricula are scoped to a family; there is no generic one-size curriculum.
  • Drafting and active versions are kept, with a snapshot of the guiding light used.
  • Assessment follows the platform's fair-scoring rules.
How it works

Step by step

Profile

Family, guardians, children and goals, combined or per child.

Interview

The agent asks about vision before planning anything.

Objectives

Why each subject is taught and what it should achieve.

Features

What shapes the plan

Main guiding light

Free text, and heavily weighted in every agent decision.

Guardian profiles

Mother tongue, budget, location and city facilities, likes, dislikes and goals.

Per-child skills

Choose skills to develop; a shared registry is tracked separately for each child.

Curriculum components

Objectives, content, instruction and assessment, with grading standards stated up front.

FAQ

Common questions

How long does the interview take?

It is a conversation, usually a handful of exchanges, and you can come back to refine the plan later.

Can I change the guiding light?

Yes. A new curriculum can be drafted from an updated profile.

Is the curriculum tied to a national standard?

Not by default. It is built from your goals, though you can ask the agent to align to a framework you name.
