Beyond chat: tools for running real projects

Recent releases add the features managers asked for most. Pricing and availability are shown below.

Meeting answers

A short answer to say aloud, a delivery cue and deeper follow-ups with dated, sourced evidence tiles. Statistics are accepted only when they match real search citations.

Commitment ledger

Reads meeting transcripts, Slack, Jira and ClickUp to catch verbal commitments that never landed anywhere, and flags drift between what was said and what the tracker shows.

Goals

Give the assistant a durable objective with a token budget. It keeps working across many turns, checkpoints its progress and stops when it can show the work is done.

Checks

Write a question once, such as whether a contract renews automatically, and get a probability, a pick or a score for every document, with flagged results queued for review.

Skills

Save how you like recurring work done, including release notes or client reports, and the assistant follows it whenever a request matches. Import Claude-style SKILL.md files.

Video and 3D animation

Generate narrated videos and standalone 3D animations. Scenes are written as code by your chosen model, checked and repaired automatically, and rendered offline.
